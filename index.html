Surf IQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Surf IQ is an offline tide and surf-session application for compatible Garmin watches. The publisher is Surf IQ. For questions about this policy, contact richya1@hotmail.com.

Tide browsing and preferences

The app calculates tides on the watch from bundled astronomical data. It stores selected beaches, favourites, settings, optional surf ratings and calculated tide results locally. A phone connection, account with Surf IQ or external tide server is not required to use these calculations.

Surf-session recording

When you start a recording, the app uses the watch's GPS and available motion/sensor information to record the activity and calculate session statistics. This can include location, time, speed, distance, movement and inferred activity states such as paddling or riding. The app creates a Garmin activity recording and keeps a bounded local history of session summaries and diagnostic samples. These classifications are estimates.

You control recording through Start, Pause, Resume, Save and Discard. Pausing stops the app's continuous GPS requests and tracking. Saving retains the Garmin activity and a local summary. Discarding an unsaved recording does not save a session summary. The app rotates its local summary storage across three slots; this does not delete activities saved separately by Garmin.

Sharing and synchronisation

Surf IQ does not operate an app backend, transmit your watch data to the publisher, or include advertising or third-party analytics tracking in the watch app. Saved Garmin activities may synchronise to Garmin Connect and services you have connected to Garmin, according to your Garmin settings. Garmin's processing is governed by its own privacy policy and account controls.

An optional diagnostic export writes session information to the device/application log. If you choose to share exported logs with support, they may contain session timestamps and activity information. Review them before sharing.

Your controls

You can change preferences and favourites in the app, choose not to record, pause a recording, or discard an unsaved recording. Use Garmin's device, activity and account controls to manage saved recordings and synchronised copies. Removing watch data does not necessarily remove copies already synchronised to Garmin or another service.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the publisher receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Do not send passwords or signing keys. Contact the same address if you want to discuss or request removal of information you have sent directly to support.

This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, forms or cookies added by Surf IQ. GitHub may process technical information when serving the page under its own privacy statement. Visiting this page is separate from using offline tide calculations on the watch.

Changes

This page will be updated if Surf IQ's data handling changes. The date above identifies this version.
